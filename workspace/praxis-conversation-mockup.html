Top picks

  • Sony WH-1000XM5 — $279. The current benchmark. Multipoint pairing, speak-to-chat, best plane-noise suppression at this price. Folds flat. No IP rating is the only downside.
  • Bose QuietComfort 45 — $249. Softer ANC but warmer sound and better call quality. More comfortable for long flights. Frequently dips to $199 on sale.
  • Anker Soundcore Q45 — $80. Budget pick. ANC is weaker on aircraft but impressive for the price. 50hr battery, no multipoint.

What to skip

Beats Studio Pro ($349) exceeds budget and trails Sony on ANC. JBL Live 770NC has audible ANC hiss and a poor companion app.

Buy the WH-1000XM5. It frequently drops to $249 on Amazon. If you want to save $80 and can tolerate softer ANC, the QC45 is a solid second.
