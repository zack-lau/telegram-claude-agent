## 0. The one hard rule — file Singapore FIRST (Section 34)

Because the inventor is Singapore-resident and the invention was made in Singapore, **Section 34 of the Singapore Patents Act** forbids first-filing abroad without clearance. Two compliant paths:

- **Path A (recommended): File in Singapore first**, then file the US provisional **after the Singapore filing** (you may file the US provisional immediately after the SG filing is lodged; the simplest fully-safe reading is to file SG first and US thereafter, or wait the 2-month period — confirm timing with your SG agent). No separate clearance needed if no prohibition order issues.
- **Path B: Obtain written national-security clearance** (IPOS "Application for Clearance under S.34", **no fee**) **before** any overseas filing, then you may file US first. Clearance is routine for consumer sleep-wellness subject matter.

**Do NOT file the US provisional first without Path B clearance** — it is a criminal offence under s.34 (compoundable, but also creates downstream risk).

US **35 USC 184 foreign-filing license is NOT required** here, because the invention was made in Singapore, not the US.

## Phase B — US provisional (USPTO), after Phase A

| # | Action | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | Confirm Phase A is done (or S.34 clearance obtained) | Gate — see §0 |
| B2 | File via **USPTO Patent Center** (patentcenter.uspto.gov) | Electronic; no non-electronic surcharge for provisionals |
| B3 | **Provisional Application for Patent Cover Sheet (SB/16)** | Names inventor(s), title, applicant |
| B4 | Upload **specification PDF** + **drawings** | `provisional_filing_copy.pdf` — informal mermaid figures are acceptable under 35 USC 111(b) |
| B5 | Pay **provisional filing fee (37 CFR 1.16(d))** | **$325** large / **$130** small / **$65** micro (eff. 2025-01-19) — see entity note below |
| B6 | (Optional) claim **Paris Convention priority to the SG filing** | Lets you anchor US priority to the earlier SG date; or file US as its own first filing |
| B7 | Receive US provisional application number + filing date | 12-month clock to convert |

**US note — no claims required.** A provisional needs only §112(a) written description + enablement (MPEP 601); the included 17 claims are a bonus that strengthens priority support. No formal drawings, no oath/declaration, no IDS at the provisional stage.

## US entity status (pick before B5)

| Status | Provisional fee | Who qualifies |
|---|---|---|
| Micro | **$65** | Small-entity AND gross income < ~$251,190 (2025) for each applicant/inventor/owner AND named on ≤4 prior US non-provisionals |
| Small | **$130** | "Small business concern" (<500 employees), independent inventor, or nonprofit — foreign entities may qualify |
| Large | **$325** | Default if neither above |

Prostec Labs likely qualifies as **small entity**; possibly **micro** if its gross income is under the threshold. Verify micro-entity eligibility (it is certified under penalty) before claiming it.
