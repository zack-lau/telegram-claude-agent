## 1. Why reposition

The current Prostec identity was built as a **cold deep-tech/hardware** signal: the graphite "Signal Square" mark, *"no gradients, no accent colour, no blue,"* Helvetica Neue Bold all-caps, engineering precision for investor credibility.

But the actual product portfolio tells a different, more coherent story:

| Product | Domain | The shared truth |
|---|---|---|
| **Cove** | Sleep | Edge AI, **no cloud at runtime**, biometric-adaptive, bedside |
| **Praxis** | Cognitive load / ADHD | Trust partner, on-device-first, no guilt mechanics, privacy carve-outs |
| **Ojavi** | Nutrition | Selfie as **sensor not surveillance**, "signal not score," warmth earns trust |

All three are **privacy-first, calm, anti-surveillance, "signal-not-score"** experiences. The thread isn't "wellness." It's sharper than that:

> **Edge AI for the human body — intelligence that works close to you, with privacy and security as first-class design constraints.**

Edge AI is not just a technical substrate here. It *is* the wellness promise. In a world where every health app treats your body data as inventory, "we minimise what leaves, protect everything that does, and never treat your data as a product" is a trust position competitors structurally cannot copy without rebuilding. That is Prostec's whitespace.

---

## 2. The synthesis: honoring the founder manifesto

The original name philosophy — **Prostec = Prosthetic + Technology, "building limbs for AI"** — does *not* need to be abandoned. It needs one reframe:

- **Old surface layer:** "Professional technology" (cold, B2B, credibility-first).
- **New surface layer:** "Intelligence that lives with you" (warm, human, trust-first).
- **Deepest layer (unchanged):** Prostec builds the bridge between intelligence and the body.

The beautiful part: **edge AI literally completes the prosthetic metaphor.** A prosthetic is not a distant cloud service — it's a part of you, attached, local, always there, extending what your body can do. Edge AI *is* AI as a prosthetic sense: a limb of intelligence that lives at the edge, on your device, close to the body. The manifesto and the reposition are the same idea, finally expressed warmly.

> Augmentation, not replacement. Trusted, not extractive. With you, not watching you.

## 3. Architecture recommendation — Endorsed brands (hybrid)

**You asked me to advise. Recommendation: move from "house of brands" toward an *endorsed* model.**

Three options on the spectrum:

1. **House of brands (current):** Cove, Praxis, Ojavi each stand alone; "Prostec" lives only in legal fine print.
2. **Branded house:** Everything becomes "Prostec Sleep / Prostec Focus / Prostec Skin." One name, maximum parent equity.
3. **Endorsed (recommended):** Products keep their own distinct, audience-tuned identities **and** carry a consistent, *visible* "by Prostec" endorsement, unified by a shared design system.

**Why endorsed, not the current house-of-brands:**

- The three products share a deep, ownable thread (private edge AI for the body). **That thread is Prostec's equity.** Hiding Prostec in fine print throws that equity away — every product earns trust from scratch.
- Wellness + privacy is fundamentally a **trust compounding** play. A visible parent that consistently stands for "your body data stays on your device" makes the *fourth* product's trust cheaper to win. House-of-brands forfeits that compounding.

**Why endorsed, not full branded-house:**

- Cove (sleep calm), Praxis (ADHD trust, no-guilt), and Ojavi (fresh, warm nutrition) are tuned to **different audiences with different emotional needs**. Their distinct identities are already well-developed and shouldn't be flattened into "Prostec X."
- Forcing one name onto an ADHD trust-partner and a bedside sleep puck and a morning skin ritual would dilute all three.

**So:** distinct product brands + a consistent visible Prostec endorsement ("Cove — by Prostec" / a shared endorsement lockup) + shared design DNA (palette logic, type, motion, voice principles below). This is the Oura/Nothing/early-Google-hardware register: warm, premium, restrained, recognizably one family.

**Trademark flag:** `prostec-cove/brand/trademark-research.md` notes cease-and-desist risk from *Prostec Laboratories (AU)*. Making Prostec *more* visible raises the stakes — worth a clearance check before committing budget to a Prostec-forward consumer push. (Product names like Cove/Praxis/Ojavi are unaffected.)

## 5. Brand pillars

1. **Privacy as Architecture.** Privacy is not a policy or a feature — it is how we build. We use the best AI available, including cloud models. What we control: what data moves, why it moves, how it's protected in transit and at rest, and what never happens to it — never sold, never used to train models without consent, never treated as a product. Minimise what leaves; protect everything that does.
2. **Warmth earns trust.** Precise but never clinical. Reassure before you inform. (Directly from Ojavi's "warmth earns trust"; Praxis's "trust partner, not tool.")
3. **Signal, not score.** Give meaning, not metrics to obsess over. No guilt, no streaks, no surveillance numbers. (Ojavi + Praxis, verbatim principles.)
4. **Augmentation, not replacement.** AI extends the human and works *with* the body — the prosthetic manifesto, kept.

## 6. Voice & tone

Synthesized from the three products' existing, validated voices (Ojavi "knowledgeable friend / friend test"; Praxis "competent, brief, plain, no sycophancy, states assumptions").

- **Calm, plain, precise.** Short sentences. No hype, no exclamation theatre.
- **Warm but not perky.** Optimistic without bubbliness. "Grounded without being clinical."
- **No woo, no hype.** No crystals-and-adaptogens energy; no "AI-powered!!!" screaming. AI-powered, not AI-obsessed.
- **The friend test.** Would a knowledgeable friend say this? "You might want more omega-3s this week" passes; "Analysis indicates deficiency risk" fails.
- **Trust through restraint.** No sycophancy, no praise-baiting, no guilt. State what's assumed; express uncertainty honestly.
- **Privacy stated, not buried.** "Privacy as Architecture" is a headline, not a footnote.

**Aesthetic descriptors (Pomelli — adopt):** Organic · Soft-Futurism · Human-Centric · Fluid
**Tone words (Pomelli — adopt):** Calm · Secure · Empathetic

## 7. Visual identity directions

The reposition deliberately reverses the legacy *"no colour, no gradient"* rule — but keeps its **discipline**. Warmth comes from warm neutrals + **one** living accent, not a loud rainbow (matches Praxis "muted, low-saturation" and Ojavi "precision without coldness"). Restraint is inherited; coldness is not.

### 7a. Logo / mark — three concepts

1. **The Edge (recommended).** A soft-cornered aperture/frame with a single living dot or pulse held *inside* the boundary. Encodes the position literally: intelligence held at the edge, on the device, close to you — not floating in a cloud. This is an evolution-in-spirit of the old "Signal Square" (signal at the core of a device) but **warmed**: rounded corners, an off-center living mark, room to breathe, built to animate (the dot can pulse like breath/heartbeat).
2. **The Living Signal / Pulse.** A minimal geometric abstraction of a biosignal (breath or heart-rate waveform). Ties straight to "close to the body." Inherently animatable; strong as an app-launch/loading motif across all three products.
3. **The Threshold.** A simple aperture or arc — Prostec as the membrane between intelligence and the body. Most abstract; most flexible as an endorsement stamp, but says the least on its own.

**Recommendation: Concept 1 (The Edge).** It carries the strategy with zero explanation, bridges from the existing Signal Square heritage (so the change reads as maturation, not amnesia), and gives each product a "home" for its own accent color inside the same frame.

### 7b. Colour — three palette directions

**Direction A — "Warm Ink + Dawn" (recommended)**
- Base / ink: warm near-black (e.g. `#161412`) — a graphite *with* warmth, not the old blue-black.
- Paper: warm off-white (e.g. `#F6F1EA`) — the "gentle exhale."
- Living accent: dawn clay / amber (e.g. `#D98A5B`) — sunrise, skin, the body.
- Calm secondary: muted sage (e.g. `#8FA68E`) — breath, wellness.
- *Why:* warm neutrals (Calm/Headspace) + a single living hue (Oura premium-warmth). Skin-tone-equity friendly for Ojavi. Premium without sterile.

**Direction B — "Quiet Bio"**
- Deep calm slate-green base + one soft luminous teal/green signal accent. Risk: green is crowded in health.

**Direction C — "Near-skin + Pulse"**
- Warm neutrals + a single coral/pulse-red accent (heartbeat). Energetic; watch that it doesn't read clinical-alert.

**Per-product accents within the family:** each product owns one accent slot inside the shared frame — e.g. Cove deep night-calm, Praxis muted focus tone, Ojavi warm dawn — so they're visibly siblings, not clones.

### 7c. Typography

- **Move off cold Helvetica all-caps.** Use a **humanist grotesque** for the wordmark — precise but with warmth and life (the genre of Inter / Söhne / GT America / Aeonik; pick in a type pass).
- **Wordmark case: mixed-case "Prostec,"** not all-caps. (Your own brand-philosophy doc calls mixed-case "the sweet spot" — versatile, modern, friendly.) All-caps reserved for the most corporate/B2B contexts only.
- One type family, generous line-height, large sizes — inherits Praxis/Ojavi's calm, readable, accessible defaults.

### 7d. Motion & feel

- **Breath, not flash.** Slow, soft transitions (<0.5s for feedback; gentle ambient breathing for the mark). Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` everywhere (a hard requirement across all three products).
- The living dot/pulse "breathes" — one shared signature motion the whole family uses.

## 8. What to keep vs. retire

**Keep:**
- The name **Prostec** and its deepest manifesto layer (augmentation, the bridge to the body).
- The *discipline* of the old identity (restraint, precision, one idea well-executed).
- The well-tuned individual product identities (Cove / Praxis / Ojavi).

**Retire:**
- "Cold deep-tech/hardware" as the surface positioning.
- Graphite-only / "no colour, no gradient, no blue" as an absolute rule.
- Helvetica Neue Bold all-caps as the locked wordmark.
- House-of-brands invisibility for Prostec → move to endorsed.
