# Prostec Labs Pte. Ltd. — Brand Identity Notes

## Company
- Prostec Labs Pte. Ltd., Singapore
- Deep tech / hardware company
- Product lines: Relay (audio summaries), Sleep, Climate, Light, Cove (product)

## Logo Concept Exploration (April 2026)

### Three Initial Concepts
1. **Concept 01 — Bracket Mark**: Right-angle brackets forming a structural "P". Engineering/drafting vibe. Precision without ornament.
2. **Concept 02 — Signal Square**: Three nested squares with outer chamfered corner, center solid. Reads as signal at the core of a physical device.
3. **Concept 03 — Tile Mark**: "PT" as negative space on a black tile. Pure geometry. Debossable, silkscreenable, pixel-perfect at 16px. Colour direction: monochrome default (graphite #1E1E1E), optional accents — midnight slate (#1C2433), oxide red (#B83A2B, alert), warm sand (#7A6A52, earthy). No gradients, no blue.

### Team Votes on Initial Concepts
- **Zack**: Concept 02 (Signal Square)
- **Nicole (agent)**: Concept 02 — best balance of hardware credibility, scalability, distinctiveness, company-level seriousness. Investors/partners would trust it fastest.
- **Mira (agent)**: Concept 03 — cleanest, best favicon scaling, practical for hardware
- **Luna (agent)**: Concept 03 — most ownable and brandable, strongest for real hardware surfaces
- **Zero (agent)**: Concept 03 — most production-ready, works on CNC enclosures, thermal decals, packaging, patches, laser-etched serial plates

Result: Concept 02 selected (Zack's call), moved to extended exploration.

### Extended Exploration (Signal Square)

**Mark Geometry** — 8 variants explored: original, heavy weight, hairline, four rings, no chamfer, deep chamfer, open center, inverted fill

**Wordmark Typography** — 6 options explored. Team unanimously recommended: ALL CAPS, medium tracking.

**Dark Field Lockups** — 4 variants. Team unanimously recommended: original dark.

**Sub-Brand System** — Mark stays constant, only wordmark suffix changes:
- PROSTEC RELAY
- PROSTEC CLIMATE
- PROSTEC LIGHT
- PROSTEC COVE (product)

Everyone approved. Nicole flagged: define whether suffixes are categories vs products. Luna: use sub-brands sparingly.

### Hybrid Combinations

Designer created hybrids to address uniqueness concerns (Zero's feedback about nested squares being common):

1. **Hybrid A — Notch + C-Ring**: Rectangular notch on outer square + gap on inner ring, aligned to same corner (top-right). Compass rose / orientation system feel.
2. **Hybrid B — Notch + Offset**: Notch anchors top-right, inner ring and center dot shift toward bottom-left. Maximum directional tension. Strongest center of mass.
3. **Hybrid C — All Three Combined**: Original chamfer + inner ring gap + offset center. Maximum expression but risks noise.

### Final Recommendation (Unanimous)

**Primary Mark**: Hybrid B (dark) — Notch + Offset
- Pin/notch outer + offset inner ring and center
- "Engineered asymmetry" — notch pulls one way, mass pulls the other
- Feels anchored, not floating

**Physical/Small Fallback**: Hybrid A (Notch + C-Ring)
- Heavy weight for physical emboss, silkscreen, tiny manufacturing contexts

**Typography**: ALL CAPS, Helvetica Neue 75 (Bold), zero tracking

**Primary Colour**: #1E1E1E (graphite), no gradients, no accent colour at launch

**Caveat**: Test Hybrid B at 16px/favicon — if offset + notch collapse at small scale, fall back to Hybrid A for digital.

### Team Comments on Hybrids
- **Nicole**: Agrees with Hybrid B. The notch gives identity, offset gives tension. Ownable without overdesigned. Test at 16px, favicon, laser etch before locking.
- **Luna**: Agrees. Hybrid B has most distinct personality without getting messy. Hybrid C is "the kind of version designers love for 5 minutes and regret later."
- **Zero**: Agrees. Calls it "engineered asymmetry." Center of mass feeling matters for hardware — logo feels anchored. Test at 16px.
- **Mira**: Agrees. Designer cooked with this one.
