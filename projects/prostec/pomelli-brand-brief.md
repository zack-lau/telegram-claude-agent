## Business

**Company:** Prostec Labs Pte. Ltd.
**Location:** Singapore
**Website:** prostec.ai
**Category:** Edge AI wellbeing — consumer technology

**What we do:** We build AI-powered wellness products where privacy is architecture, not afterthought. We use the best AI available — including cloud models — but we minimise what data leaves, protect everything that does, and never treat your data as a product. No surveillance, no selling, no training on your body without consent.

**Products:**
- **Cove** — bio-adaptive acoustic sleep system. A bedside device that learns your sleep patterns and plays personalised noise profiles. Edge AI, no internet required at runtime.
- **Praxis** — AI agent app for people with ADHD. Voice-first. You dump a raw thought; the AI turns it into a finished work product. No guilt mechanics, no streaks.
- **Ojavi** — daily selfie → personalised nutrition nudges. The camera is a sensor, not a beauty filter or surveillance tool. 2–3 concrete food suggestions per day, not scores to obsess over. Warmth earns trust.

## Positioning

**One-liner:** Intelligence that lives with you.

**Positioning statement:** For people who want technology to understand their body without surveilling it, Prostec builds edge-AI products where privacy is architecture, not afterthought. Unlike cloud wellness apps that treat your body data as inventory, Prostec minimises what leaves, protects everything that does, and never treats your data as a product — precise, warm, and private by design.

**Category:** Edge AI wellbeing

---

## Brand Pillars

1. **Privacy as Architecture** — Privacy is not a policy or a feature — it is how we build. We use the best AI available, including cloud models. What we control: what data moves, why it moves, how it's protected, and what never happens to it — never sold, never used to train models without consent, never treated as a product.
2. **Warmth earns trust** — Precise but never clinical. Reassure before you inform.
3. **Signal, not score** — Give meaning, not metrics to obsess over. No guilt, no streaks, no surveillance numbers.
4. **Augmentation, not replacement** — AI extends the human and works with the body — not instead of it.

---

## Voice & Tone

- Calm, plain, precise. Short sentences. No hype, no exclamation marks.
- Warm but not perky. Optimistic without bubbliness.
- No wellness woo. No AI hype. AI-powered, not AI-obsessed.
- The friend test: would a knowledgeable friend say this? "You might want more omega-3s this week" passes. "Analysis indicates deficiency risk" does not.
- Privacy stated, not buried. "Stays on your device" is a headline, not a footnote.

**Tone references:** Oura Ring (premium health data, warm not sterile) + Calm/Headspace (breathing room, the feeling of a gentle exhale) + Nothing/teenage engineering (precision, restraint, no fluff)

**Anti-references:** Goop (no pseudoscience), MyFitnessPal (no obsessive tracking), generic AI apps (no "AI-powered!!!" screaming)

## Visual Direction

**Primary palette — Pomelli Primary:**
- Prussian Blue (base): `#08415C` — depth, trust, privacy
- Chalk White (surface): `#F4F4F9` — clean, open
- Warm Coral (accent): `#EF8354` — warmth, the body, life
- Latte Brown (secondary): `#CC998D` — skin-tone friendly, grounded

**Typography:** Humanist grotesque (Inter or similar). Mixed-case "Prostec" — not all-caps. Generous line height, readable at all sizes.

**Mark concept — The Edge:** A soft-cornered aperture/frame with a single living dot inside the boundary. Intelligence held at the edge, on the device, close to you. Not floating in a cloud.

**Feel:** Warm, precise, calm. Not clinical, not cold, not loud. One living accent colour, warm neutrals, restraint.
